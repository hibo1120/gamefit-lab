# GameFit Global test pack

Small-batch drafts for testing English-language demand without changing or replacing the Japanese growth pack. Nothing in this folder has been posted, scheduled, or sent to an external service.

## Contents

- `youtube-shorts.md`: 6 faceless, caption-first Shorts scripts
- `x-posts.md`: 10 X drafts (8 value-first, 2 direct diagnosis CTAs)
- `reddit-research.md`: 5 question/research drafts; community participation, not link drops

All content IDs use an `en_` prefix so Japanese and Global acquisition can be compared without collision. Links use `utm_campaign=gamefit_global_test` and the platform conventions below:

| Platform | Source | Medium | IDs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube | `youtube` | `shorts` | `en_yt_s01`–`en_yt_s06` |
| X | `x` | `social` | `en_x_01`–`en_x_10` |
| Reddit | `reddit` | `community` | `en_reddit_01`–`en_reddit_05` |

Before any manual post, re-check game/product claims, follow each community’s rules, adapt the draft to the audience, and verify the final UTM URL. Do not add affiliate links while all Global merchants remain disabled.
