Important limitations

GameFit is a simplified decision aid, not a benchmark, compatibility checker, price quote, or guarantee. Performance changes with hardware, settings, resolution, drivers and game updates. Official requirements belong to their publishers; GameFit recommendations are independent judgment. Always verify current specifications before buying.
